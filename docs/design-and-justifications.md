# Why Everything Was Changed — Vanilla Root Causes & Engineering Justifications (`v0.9.9`)

This document explains **what vanilla *Easy Red 2* (Game Build `921`, `GameAssembly.dll`) does under the hood**, **why each behavior caused gameplay, fairness, Co-op, or performance issues**, and **how [`EasyRed2HumanAI`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Plugin.cs) (`v0.9.9`, `28` modular C# files, `155` verified Harmony patches)** solves it.

---

## 1. Vision, Camouflage, Hearing, Reaction Time & Platform Optics ([`VisionRangeAndCamo.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs), [`AiCore.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.cs))

### 1.1. Stance Stealth, Night Darkness & Individual Firing Reveal (`Soldier.CamoDistanceAwareEnemies` — RVA `0x18073db30`)
* **What Vanilla Did**:
  * When a squad is unspotted (`IsEnemyAwareOfThisSquad == false`), vanilla returns `220m` (Standing), `50m` (Crouching), and `37m` (Prone).
  * However, the moment **any** member of your squad fires a shot or is spotted (`IsEnemyAwareOfThisSquad == true`), vanilla jumps `CamoDistanceAwareEnemies` to a **flat `300.0m` for every member of the squad**, regardless of whether they are standing, crouching, or crawling prone in grass.
  * Meanwhile, `DayNightCycle` + `Creature.CanSee` (`0.20 + 0.80 * NightVision`) left silent infantry visible at `115m–180m` at midnight while `AIPlane.GetBestAircraftVisibleEnemy` had zero darkness check, and adding muzzle-flash range *before* multiplying by vanilla's night factor shrank muzzle flashes instead of making gunfire stand out in darkness.
* **Why It Needed Changing**:
  * If a single AI rifleman 30 meters behind you fired a shot, every enemy bot within 300m immediately gained the ability to spot you crawling prone in tall grass (`37m -> 300m`). Night maps also offered little stealth value for silent movement and poorly rewarded night shoot-and-scoot tactics.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`Soldier_CamoDistanceAwareEnemies_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`Soldier_StartFire_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`AiCore.UpdateNightVisionSnapshotIfDue`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.cs))**:
  * Preserves stance stealth during combat (`150m` Standing, `80m` Crouching, `40m` Prone) with a modest `1.20x` squad-awareness multiplier (`48m` prone, `96m` crouched).
  * Records the **individual shooter's `IntPtr`** on weapon fire and applies a `4.0s` firing-reveal bonus (`GetFiringVisibilityBonus`, representing muzzle flash, smoke, and gunshot report) to that specific soldier only.
  * Samples `DayNightCycle.instance` once every `0.5s` (`AiCore.NightDarkness01`, zero per-bot calls in `CanSee`) so silent targets shrink to realistic WWII night ranges while active muzzle flares pop cleanly inside the observer's sight cone (`EnableRealisticNightDarkness`).

### 1.2. Continuous Human "Squint" Vision Curves, Tank Periscope & Armor X-Ray Leak (`Creature.CanSee` — RVA `0x180a4afb0` & `TurretAI.CanSee`)
* **What Vanilla Did**:
  * In `Creature.CanSee`, vanilla only checked `AngleToDirection <= 60°` when `dist >= 30m` **AND `!this.isAlerted`**. Once alerted or suppressed (`isAlerted == true`), vanilla skipped the angle check completely, granting **360-degree omnidirectional vision out to 300m**—including to buttoned-up tank gunners peering through fixed hull/turret slits.
  * Meanwhile, `TurretAI.AutoPilot` (`0x180834000`) never rotated `TurretCommander` when `CurrentVisibleTarget == null` (leaving the commander staring straight along the hull while `TurretAI.CanSee` gave him a 360° bubble).
  * Furthermore, in `Soldier.GetBestVisibleEnemy` (`0x18074ADC0`), Crewman #1 inside a tank was converted to the `Vehicle`, leaving `CurrentVisibleTarget != null`—so Crewman #2+ fell into the foot-soldier branch where `Creature.CanSee` raycasts against `groundMask` (`0x101`, which **excludes vehicle armor colliders**), letting AI riflemen see and shoot buttoned-up tank crew straight through `80mm` armor plate!
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`Creature_CanSee_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`TurretAI_CanSee_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`TurretAI_AutoPilot_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/ImmersionAndQoL/VeterancyAndPersonalities.cs), [`AiCore.EvaluateHumanSquintHalfFov`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.cs))**:
  * **Continuous Human Distance "Squint" (`Creature.CanSee`)**: Replaces binary FOV cliffs with a continuous distance curve (`<= 12m: 360°` close awareness; `12m–34m: ±136° -> ±90°` with dual body + barrel angle checks; `34m–75m: ±90° -> ±56°`; `75m–210m+: ±56° -> ±32°` focused long-range gaze) plus an O(1) guard rejecting buttoned-up crew (`!seat.showUnitSet && !veh.IsCoverDestroyed()`) on armored ground hulls (`effArmor >= 8mm`) so small arms never X-ray through steel plate.
  * **Active 360° Commander Periscope & Platform-Distinct Turret Optics (`TurretAI.CanSee` & `TurretAI.AutoPilot`)**: Rotates `TurretCommander` toward recent enemy gunfire (`<= 185m`) or sweeps 360° at `55°/s` with a directional periscope FOV (`78° -> 62° -> 42°`), `1.45x` binocular boost, and `11.5m` hull-depression dead zone; **Enclosed Tank Gunners** peer through a narrow **`±28°–34° -> ±15°–19°` gun slit** fed via a **`0.65s–1.48s` Commander & infantry-escort intercom handoff** (`AiCore.GetCachedVehicleSpotterStatus`); **Static AT/Field Guns** obey their gun shield (`<= 8.5m` close ear awareness + forward arc); and **AA Mounts** scan wide `±90°` sky sectors.

### 1.3. Scope & Turret Optic Magnification Scaling (`Creature.CanSee` & `TurretAI.CanSee`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: A sniper with a 4x–8x scope or a tank gunner peering through magnified optics used the exact same `target.CamoDistance()` as an un-scoped SMG trooper.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`AiCore.GetCachedOpticZoom`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.cs), [`VisionRangeAndCamo.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs))**: Queries `heldGun.AimingFOV()` (or turret optic FOV) and scales sight range by `60f / opticFov` (capped at `2.25x`) strictly inside a narrow `25°` half-angle optic cone.

### 1.4. Stationary Threat-Sector Gaze & Window-Aperture Alignment (`SoldierAI.SequentialUpdate`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: Once a stationary defender finished moving (`StopMove`), vanilla never updated their facing direction until a target was already inside their sight cone, enforced a `2.5s` `alertStartTime` freeze before acquiring targets beyond `30m`, and left indoor bots staring parallel to interior bedroom walls instead of out windows. Also, `Soldier.AngleToDirection` had a `90°` coordinate mismatch on gaze angles.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`CenterGazeInWindowAperture`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs), [`TryGetKnownThreatSectorDirection`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs), [`Soldier_AngleToDirection_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.cs))**: Orients stationary defenders toward known enemy contact sectors (`175m`), aligns indoor defenders cleanly onto **Window Apertures** (wall at windowsill height + clear exterior view at eye height), fixes `AngleToDirection` / `SignedAngleToDirection`, and scales `alertStartTime` to dynamic human reaction curves (`0.18s–0.32s` watching vs. `0.38s–0.65s+` caught out).

### 1.5. Local `15m` Contact Callouts & Last-Known Suppressive Fire / Grenades
* **What Vanilla Did**: When a target ducked behind a wall or crater lip (`CanSee == false`), bots immediately stopped firing and forgot the contact position.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`RecordAndBroadcastVisualContact`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs), [`TryMaintainSuppressiveFireOnLastKnown`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs), [`TryFlushLastKnownEnemyWithGrenade`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs))**: Shares a `12s` threat-sector gaze vector (never X-ray targeting) with nearby friendlies (`15m`), lets stationary MGs/automatic rifles fire a suppressive burst at unobstructed cover edges (`CanDeliverSuppressiveFireTowardPoint`), and lets nearby infantry flush known cover with a frag grenade (`IsSafeToThrowGrenadeAt`).

### 1.6. Adaptive AI Brain Tick Scheduler (`Creature.ProcessSequentialUpdates` — RVA `0x180a53760`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: `Creature.ProcessSequentialUpdates` processes **1 slot** of `Creature.allCreatures` per frame—leaving bots waiting `2.0–2.5+ seconds` between brain ticks in `120–140` unit battles, or wasting entire frames whenever `sequentialUpdateIndex` lands on a `Vehicle`, dead slot, or seated passenger (`Soldier.IsOnVehicle()`).
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`Creature_ProcessSequentialUpdates_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.cs))**: Dynamically scales active on-foot `SoldierAI` brain ticks per frame with population (`1` tick at `<= 45` units, `2` at `46–95`, `3` at `96+`), skips non-soldier / passenger slots cleanly, and backs off to vanilla `1` tick/frame automatically whenever `Time.unscaledDeltaTime > 0.025f` (`< 40 FPS`), dropping `140`-bot reaction latency from `2.0–2.5s` down to `~0.6s` with zero extra Photon traffic.

### 1.7. Close-Range Footstep & Gunfire Hearing (`<= 20m`) + Battlefield Noise Masking (`Soldier.TryPlayStepSound` — RVA `0x18076C470`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: Bots had no true footstep acoustics (`dist <= 10m` in `CanSee` bypassed FOV silently regardless of stance/movement, giving away sneaking prone flankers at close range while failing to react to a sprinting enemy coming up behind them).
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`Soldier_TryPlayStepSound_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`RecordSoldierFootstep`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`TryGetNearestCloseAcousticThreat`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`TryExecuteCloseAcousticInvestigate`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Goap/SoldierGoapExecution.cs))**: Hooks real footfalls via a zero-allocation ring buffer detecting standing sprints (`<= 17m`) and standing walks (`<= 9.5m`) while keeping crouched and prone movement completely silent, cuts hearing range by `50%` whenever gunfire or explosions occur within `35m` in the last `3s` (`HasAnyLoudEventNear`), triggers alert snap-turns toward close footsteps/gunfire (`<= 20m`), and dispatches at most **one** unengaged squad investigator (`AcousticInvestigatorPtr`) without breaking squad formation.

## 2. Infantry Combat, Stance, Cover, Medics & CQB ([`StanceAndCoverTactics.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs), [`LocomotionAndVaulting.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/LocomotionAndVaulting.cs), [`CloseCombatAndSupport.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`FriendlyFireAndYield.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/FriendlyFireAndYield.cs))

### 2.1. Committed Fire-and-Movement Locomotion, `"Shot-At"` Drop & Low-Wall / Trench / Pillbox Peeking
* **What Vanilla Did**: `SoldierAI.get_IsMoveTime` (`0x180562080`) ran on a stateless modulo clock (`((Time.time + timeSeed) % 7.0f) < 2.5f`), `SoldierAI.FixedUpdate` (`0x18055A504`) overwrote `moveCharacter` every physics tick, `ShouldLookTargetWhileMoving` forced 50% sideways crab-strafe shooting, `NodesCount == 1` dropped final approach segments to walk speed, and `DestinationWithoutCover..ctor` rolled a **50% chance to force `Prone`** whenever no cover node existed—creating stuttery `crouch -> 1-step run -> crouch` loops.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`SoldierAI_IsMoveTime_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/LocomotionAndVaulting.cs), [`SoldierAI_MovementRoutine_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/ThreatMemoryAndWatchSectors.cs), [`SoldierAI_SequentialUpdate_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs))**:
  * **Committed Fire-and-Movement (`FireAndMovementState`)**: Hysteresis-locked **upright sprints (`4.8s–6.8s`)** into cover alternated with **deliberate kneeling firing halts (`3.7s–5.35s`)**, continuing smoothly across 1–3 frame `IsAsyncPathRefreshPending` gaps (`HasPathRequest`), restricting moving fire to forward CQB (`<= 8.5m` / `<= 16m` charge within `52°`), and adding blind-corner pieing (`TryGetActiveCornerPieDirection`, capped at `<= 115°` and disabled during evacuations).
  * **`"Shot-At"` Startle Drop & Low-Wall / Trench / Pillbox Peeking**: `SoldierAI_OnSuppressed_Patch` expires GOAP `NextReplanTime` after `0.16s–0.43s` human startle, drops open-ground runners to cover, ducks bots behind waist-high walls between firing bursts, runs staggered trench sentry peeks, and protects pillbox/attic lintels.

### 2.2. Indoor Ceiling Prone Prevention (`EnableIndoorCeilingPronePrevention`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: Suppressed or defending soldiers inside buildings, bunkers, and pillboxes dropped `Prone` directly underneath windowsills (`0.8m` high) or on staircases, blinding themselves.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`AiCore.HasLowCeilingOverhead`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.WorldQuery.cs), [`SetBotPose`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs))**: Uses a quantized `1m × 1m` spatial cache (`30s` TTL) over `GetSolidEnvironmentMask()` up to `4.2m` overhead and locks indoor soldiers to functional `Crouch` stances (except under exterior crossfire).

### 2.3. Gunfire-Driven Leeward Cover, Crossfire Shelter & Tank Hull Cover (`CombatCover.IsCoverAvailable`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: `CombatCover` nodes along walls frequently placed defenders on the **enemy-facing side of the wall**, and multi-directional crossfire caused single-wall bots to ping-pong back and forth across a wall.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`CombatCover_IsCoverAvailable_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs), [`IsPositionOnExposedSideOfWall`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs), [`TryGetDominantEnemyGunfireDirection`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Vision/VisionRangeAndCamo.cs), [`TryFindTankHullCoverOrEscortSpot`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs))**:
  * **Leeward Cover & Circular-Coherence Crossfire (`R < 0.82f`)**: Prioritizes cover relative to the bot's active target first, detects multi-direction crossfire (`shooterCount >= 2 && R < 0.82f`) to stop wall-flipping ping-pong, awards roofed interior cover (`HasLowCeilingOverhead`) a `+34f` bonus and allows `Prone` shelter indoors/in trenches under crossfire, and enables **Tank Hull Cover & Escort** behind friendly armor.

### 2.4. Dual Barrel-Height Wall-Shooting Prevention, Auto-Peek & Doorway Jamb Sidesteps (`SoldierAI.RotateAndFireToward`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: `Creature.CanSee` raycasted from head/eyes whereas bullets spawn from the lower **weapon barrel**, causing bots behind low walls or doorway jambs to dump magazines straight into stone/brick.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`IsMuzzleBlockedByWall`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/FriendlyFireAndYield.cs), [`SoldierAI_RotateAndFireToward_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs), [`TrySidestepWallObstruction`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/StanceAndCoverTactics.cs))**: Checks `AiCore.RaycastEnvironment` from spine and `heldGun.transform.position`; steps up one stance (`Prone -> Crouch -> Stand`) over low cover or sidesteps `0.90m–1.85m` around jambs and vertical corners.

### 2.5. Point-Blank Bayonet / Un-Bayoneted Melee Reach, Anti-Spin Pincers & Safe Bandaging
* **What Vanilla Did**:
  * `Soldier.MeleeDamageCR` (`0x18077EB28`) used `dist = 0.37f` (`0.67m` total reach with its `0.30f`-radius `OverlapCapsule`) whenever `mountedBlade == null`—falling short of bots' `0.88m–0.96m` standoff so pistol-whips, buttstrokes, and knives whiffed endlessly.
  * Standing swings missed crouched/prone targets (`FacePosition.y` too high), `MoveDirectlyToward` spun attackers on top of targets, `boltActionRequired` sent riflemen into `<= 15m` gunfire, vanilla `< 1.6m` checks forced melee swings into static-gun tripods, and `TryStopBleeding` holstered rifles to bandage in front of shooters.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`CloseCombatAndSupport.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`Soldier_MeleeDamageCR_MoveNext_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`CanSafelyBandageNow`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs))**:
  * Mounts integrated bayonets cleanly without RPC bursts (`TryEnsureBayonetMounted`), extends AI un-bayoneted swing reach in `MeleeDamageCR` to `dist = 0.95f` (`1.45m` across static-gun tripods), scales weapon-specific melee standoffs (`1.30m` bayonet / `0.96m` knife / `0.88m` pistol-whip), keeps loaded pistols firing down to `0.72m` and exposed emplacement gunners shot point-blank (`TryResolvePointBlankTargetSoldier`), engages a `4.0s` `MeleeWhiffFallbackUntil` firearm lockout after `3` consecutive whiffed swings, caps attackers to `2` per target on `±52°` pincers, matches crouch/prone stance (`GetDesiredMeleePose`), and blocks bandaging while enemies have close LOF (`CanSafelyBandageNow`).

### 2.6. Strategic Door & Window Shutter Discipline (`Soldier.CheckAndOpenDoor` — RVA `0x180743970`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: Uncachedly called `GetComponent<InteragibleDoor>()` on every collider in range and unconditionally called `SetDoor(true)`, popping open closed window shutters and never closing trailing doors (`SetDoor(false)`).
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`Soldier_CheckAndOpenDoor_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/LocomotionAndVaulting.cs))**: Reuses the existing `Collider[]` array (`0.30s` throttle, cached `Collider.Pointer`), opens doors only along the travel/firing lane, closes trailing doors behind the squad (`!IsAnySquadmateApproachingDoor`), and pulls shutters **closed** when pinned (`suppression >= 32f`), bleeding, or reloading.

### 2.7. Bridge & Narrow Crossing Discipline (`SoldierAI.GetSpacingFromSquad` — RVA `0x18055af40`)
* **What Vanilla Did**: Shifted pathfinding endpoints sideways by **`Random.Range(5, 35)` meters** before `CPathFinding`, flinging waypoints off bridges into the river/ravine below.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`AiCore.IsOnBridgeOrNarrowCrossing`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.WorldQuery.cs), [`AiCore.IsSafeBridgeDeckStep`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.WorldQuery.cs), [`SoldierAI_GetSpacingFromSquad_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/LocomotionAndVaulting.cs))**: Quantized `2m × 1m × 2m` bridge detection cancels `5m–35m` scatter on bridges/drop-offs (`IsBridgeSideOpenDropOff`), uses deck-safe `TryFindBridgeParapetOrPillarSpot` parapet cover and diagonal railing bipods (`HandleGunnerBipodAndWallMounting`), and routes chronically wedged hillside bots through contour probes (`TryRecoverChronicallyStuckFootBot` + `TryFindSquadHillsideContourPoint`).

### 2.8. Shell-Shock Surrender, Downed-Casualty Standing-Zombie Fix & Animator Verification (`Soldier.SurrenderSynched` — RVA `0x18076a3c0`)
* **What Vanilla Did**:
  * AI never surrendered dynamically during live combat.
  * Conversely, `BattleManager.SurrenderUnitsOnMissionAreas` (`0x180595F7D`) and `BattleManager.OnWin` (`0x1805A2B4C`) checked only `IsAlive`—ignoring `IsInjured == true`, so downed wounded casualties awaiting revive stood up in a surrender zombie pose when flags flipped! And `Soldier.SetAnimatorController` (`0x1807636F3`) aborted restoring the walking `RuntimeAnimatorController` if `surrended` (`+0x49E`) was set mid-vehicle-exit.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`TryEvaluateShellShockSurrender`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`Soldier_SurrenderSynched_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`Soldier_Surrender_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`Soldier_SetAnimatorController_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`AiCore.IsSoldierInVehicleOrTransition`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.cs))**:
  * Allows dynamic surrender strictly for isolated, un-officered Skittish (`[Cautious]`, `[Triage]`), green Rookie, or out-of-ammo non-fanatic Regular troops within `18m` of enemies (`TryLiberateNearbyComrade` later frees them).
  * Guards all foot-AI / surrender hooks against the `0.6s–1.4s` vehicle enter/exit transition window (`AiCore.IsSoldierInVehicleOrTransition`), skips `IsInjured` casualties during area/win surrenders, restores walking animators safely, uses verified native `Soldier.asm` strings (`"Surrended"`, `"Injured"`, `"StartInjured"`), and clears `visibleTarget` immediately on surrender/syringe capture (`ClearAllFriendlyTargetLocksOnSoldier`).

### 2.9. Cross-Subsystem Priority Hierarchy & Friendly-Fire Safety
* **Movement Priority Chain**: *(1) Survival, Shell-Shock Surrender / Bleeding Cover-Seek* → *(2) Point-Blank CQB Melee* → *(3) Evading Heavy Armor* → *(4) Vehicle Repair / Emplacement Staffing* → *(5) Bridge Crossing Advance* → *(6) Attacker Bounding Overwatch* → *(7) Out-of-Combat Comrade Liberation*, protecting active GOAP overrides from `SquadOrdersRoutine` rubber-banding (`TryGetProtectedBotMoveOverride`).
* **Player Crosshair Yield ([`FriendlyFireAndYield.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/FriendlyFireAndYield.cs), [`EnsurePlayerAimLanesUpdated`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.WorldQuery.cs))**: Zero-allocation `PlayerAimLane` cache rejects cover nodes in friendly player aim rays and signals nearby friendly bots (`UpdateFriendlyPlayerCrosshairYield`) to duck/step aside cleanly.

### 2.10. `InvisibleTrepassableWall` (`Layer 14`) Vaulting, Trench Drop-In & Settled Slope Physics
* **What Vanilla Did**:
  * Sandbags, low stone walls, trench parapets, and fences sit on **Layer 14 (`InvisibleTrepassableWall`)**, excluded from `ResourcesManager.groundMask` (`0x101`). Meanwhile `CPathFinding.CalculatePath` (`0x1807BB530`) rejects downward drops `> 1.12m`, leaving bots on trench rims running around ends instead of dropping in.
* **Settled Engine Invariants & Fix ([`LocomotionAndVaulting.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/LocomotionAndVaulting.cs), [`AiCore.WorldQuery.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Core/AiCore.WorldQuery.cs))**:
  * **Never call `Soldier.ForceControllerEnabled()` on grounded AI**: Vanilla `RefreshControllerEnabled` intentionally disables `CharacterController` so `Soldier.ApplyMove` moves via `Transform.Translate` + height-snapping, allowing smooth climbs up `> 45°` `TerrainCollider` slopes without hitting Unity's `45°` `slopeLimit`.
  * **Never `TryCast<TerrainCollider>()`**: `UnityEngine.TerrainPhysicsModule.dll` is stripped (`0` native methods); always use `AiCore.IsTerrainCollider(col)` and reject `Vehicle` colliders via `AiCore.IsVehicleCollider(col)`.
  * Combines `vaultMask | groundMask` in `AiCore.GetMovementObstacleMask()`, validates thin-fence crests and landing clearance (`IsValidVaultObstacle`, `HasClearVaultArcAndLanding`), and uses a 2-ray trench-lip probe (`TryStepOffIntoTrench`) so bots drop cleanly into `0.55m–2.65m` deep trenches via `MoveDirectlyToward`.

### 2.11. Medic `MedicHealingDestination` Approach & `"Less is More"` `Squad.Join` Guard
* **What Vanilla Did**: Vanilla `Squad.MedicRoutine` assigns `MedicHealingDestination` (`0x18056EC40`) where `OnCoverReached` is a no-op and `TryUseSyringeOnTarget` requires `dist < 1.5f`—often leaving medics frozen in cover `2.5m` short of the downed soldier. Meanwhile custom duplicate `Squad.Join` calls conflicted with vanilla `Squad.UpdateSquadData()`.
* **Our Justification & Fix ([`DriveMedicHealingApproach`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Goap/TeamNetAndSquadCommand.cs), [`Squad_MedicRoutine_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/Infantry/CloseCombatAndSupport.cs), [`Squad_Join_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/UI/ConfigMenuAndHud.cs), [`SyncSoldier_SyncSquad_Patch`](../src/EasyRed2AI/UI/ConfigMenuAndHud.cs))**: Drives medics actively to `< 1.5f` with `requestImmediatePath: true` and `ActiveSyringeHealUntil` anti-stuck grace; lets native `Squad.UpdateSquadData()` handle `10m` merges while enforcing `<= 18m` Squad Leader distance, `!targetLeader.IsInjured`, and incoming `SyncSoldier.SyncSquad` preservation.

## 3. Macro Objectives, Emplacements, Armor & Aircraft ([`MacroObjectiveDirector.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/MacroAndVehicles/MacroObjectiveDirector.cs), [`CombinedArmsAndTurrets.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/MacroAndVehicles/CombinedArmsAndTurrets.cs), [`GroundVehicleTactics.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/MacroAndVehicles/GroundVehicleTactics.cs), [`AircraftTactics.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/MacroAndVehicles/AircraftTactics.cs), [`VehicleRoadkillAndRightOfWay.cs`](../src/EasyRed2AI/Modules/MacroAndVehicles/VehicleRoadkillAndRightOfWay.cs))

### 3.1. Universal Objectives, Full-Army Staffing, Smoke-Screened Attrition Pivots & Coastal Spawn Rescue
* **What Vanilla Did**: Captured flags were abandoned immediately (`MissionObjective.attractor` drop), `SendUnitsToCovers` scattered defenders outside capture circles, `Squad.TaskCanStillAttract` kept squads stuck on deactivated Lua objectives, and shoreline/boat-overflow spawns could drop AI into deep water.
