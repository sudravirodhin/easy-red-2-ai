* **Vanilla Coastal, Underground & Unparachuted Spawn-Death Fix (`MacroAndVehiclesModule.cs`, `Plugin.cs`, `FeatureMenuAndHud.cs`)**:
  * **Why Vanilla Bots Repeatedly Spawned & Died on Both Coastal and Waterless Maps**: In `Soldier.FixedUpdate` (`0x18074A24C`) and `Creature.UpdateGenericValues` (`0x180A546E5`), any unseated soldier (`!IsOnVehicle()`) below `y = -1.7f` (`-2.5f` for `Creature`, checked unconditionally on every map regardless of water) triggers `KillSynched()` directly—bypassing `Damage()` and `Soldier.SetInvincible(2f..10f)`. All instantiations run through `SpawnManager._SpawnUnit_d__37.MoveNext` (`0x1806BE840`), where `GetSpawnPosition` (`0x1806AC8FD`) only casts a short `6.7m` downward ray that accepts seabed hits, misses over deep water or steep slopes, spawns below one-sided mountain `TerrainCollider` surfaces, or misses `6.5m–45m` cliffs/valleys; meanwhile `Steps 0..3` yields drop pre-seated passengers, `spawnVehicle.GetOnVehiclePosOrdered` overflows dump extra bots into offshore water or air, and unseated high-altitude spawns never set `useParachuteHeight` (`+0x300`, otherwise only assigned in `CargoPlane.GetOffVehicle`), causing `SetInvincible(2f)` to expire mid-freefall before lethal landing damage.
  * **Water-Aware, Mountain-Proof & Parachute-Armed Rescue (`SpawnManager_SpawnUnitMoveNext_Patch`)**: Uses `AiCore.TryGetActiveWaterLevel` so waterless maps preserve dry craters/basins down to `-2.35m` while guarding the global `-2.5f` void plane (`Omaha Beach` shallow surf down to `-1.30m` and seated `LandingCraft` / ramp `GetOffVehicle` dismounts remain untouched), uses `ResourcesManager.GetTerrainAltitude` so `8m–195m` searches probe from above `> 60m` mountain peaks, snaps `6.5m–45m` cliff-miss drops directly onto walkable ground (`ResetGroundLeavePos()` + zero velocity), and arms `Soldier.UseParachute(terrAlt + 450f)` on legitimate high-altitude unseated / `CargoPlane`-overflow drops (`SpawnSaved:` telemetry).
* **Human Squad Leader HQ Radial (`Q`), `[HQ]` Map Marker (`M`) & Vanilla-Friendly Callouts (`AiCore.cs`, `MacroAndVehiclesModule.cs`, `Plugin.cs`)**:
  * Replaced raw `(X, Z)` coordinate toasts with vanilla objective names (`Point A/B/C`) and rounded relative distances/compass bearings (`140m NE`).
  * Added a pooled `[HQ]` marker on the `M` tactical map (`MiniMapGUI.UpdateUnitMarkersOnMap` / `DisableRemainingUnitMarkers`) showing the active or pending HQ objective destination for human Squad Leaders and players in AI-led squads.
  * Injected **`Accept HQ: <Target>`** and **`Balk HQ Order`** directly into the root `Q` Squad Leader radial menu (`CircularMenu2.ShowCircle` + `Soldier.ProcessAction`) whenever a radio/HQ order is pending: **Accept** sets `sq.AttackPosition(pos)` (`Order.attack`) so your squadmates maneuver there without ejecting from vehicles, while **Balk** rejects the order (`65s` suppression window) and immediately triggers AI Combined-Arms / Team-Net re-tasking so another friendly unit picks up the slack.
* **AI Bot Wall-Phasing Fixes (`AiCore.cs`, `InfantryTacticsModule.cs`, `GoapAndBlackboardModule.cs`, `Plugin.cs`)**:
  * **3D Vault Arc & Interior-Snap Rejection (`HasClearVaultArcAndLanding`)**: Added an explicit crest-clearance, landing-floor, and standing-capsule sweep across all vault callsites (`SoldierAI_MovementRoutine_Patch`, `TryVaultParapetOrWall`, `TryRecoverChronicallyStuckFootBot`, and `ExecuteAnvilVaultPush`) so `Soldier.Vault` (`0x18076AD50`) never kinematic-lerps bots through solid building walls or across unwalkable voids.
  * **Chest-Thickness Obstacle `SphereCast` (`IsMovementBlockedByObstacle`)**: Upgraded thin-ray obstacle checks to a `0.20m` chest `SphereCast` plus ankle step probe to catch narrow doorframes, wall corners, and compound pillars.
  * **Direct-Steering Wall Guard (`SoldierAI_MoveDirectlyToward_Patch`)**: Guarded `SoldierAI.MoveDirectlyToward` (`0x18055BA70`) against solid walls within `2.4m`, cancelling direct-toward blind translation (`moveDirectlyToward_end = 0f`) and falling back to `UpdatePath()` when pathfinding isn't already mid-request.
  * **Unclipper Building-Interior Guard (`SoldierAI_MakeSureAiIsNotClippedInsideCollider_Patch`)**: Protected `SoldierAI.MakeSureAiIsNotClippedInsideCollider` (`0x18055B540`) from snapping wall-hugging bots across solid wall faces (`Physics.Linecast` / `CheckCapsule` against `GetSolidEnvironmentMask()`) into adjacent room interiors.
* **Truck & Co-op Remote-Client Swept Hull Roadkill Fix (`MacroAndVehiclesModule.cs`, `FeatureMenuAndHud.cs`)**:
  * **Rotation-Invariant Hull OBB Bounds (`GetCachedVehicleLocalHullObb`)**: Combined `CalculateVehicleLocalBounds()` (`WheelCollider` hubs) with non-turret low-hull solid colliders (`BoxCollider`/`MeshCollider`/`CapsuleCollider`/`SphereCollider` transformed via vehicle-local corners so `√2` world-AABB diagonal bloat and elevated tank barrels are excluded) and preserved `localCenterX/Z` pivot offsets, covering 2-axle truck front hoods, bumpers, and cargo beds.
  * **Continuous Co-op Snap-Teleport & High-Speed Swept Scan**: Captured pre/post positions across `SyncVehicle.SyncVehiclePosition` (`> 6.0m` snap-teleports on fast remote trucks) and upgraded `RunVehicleRoadkillAndRightOfWayObbScan` to a 3-axis Liang-Barsky swept local-OBB test (`sweepStart -> cur -> syncDestPos`) with anchor-relative directional checks, packet-activity fallback when a remote driver occupies a non-`0` seat, `OnTriggerEnter` offset-hull checks, and swept `FixedUpdate` scans for moving AI-driven as well as player-driven ground vehicles.
* **AI Sprint-Weight & Ammo Encumbrance Fix (`AiCore.cs`, `InfantryTacticsModule.cs`, `GoapAndBlackboardModule.cs`)**:
  * **Why Bots Got Too Heavy to Sprint (`PlayerController.Update` @ `0x1807D9722`)**: Native `InventoryManager.HasSpaceFor` (`0x1804EF200`) is a stub (`mov al, 1`), and `AddVirtualItem`, `TakeIntoInventory`, and `RefillAmmoForWearedWeaponAsync` perform zero weight checks against `GetWeightAndMaxWeight(out weight, out max_weight)`—locking sprint (`weight > max_weight`) when a human player later takes over a squadmate bot.
  * **Eliminated Mod-Side Hoarding & Async Weapon-Swap Races**: Switched all bot `RefillAmmoForWearedWeapon` calls from `discard_empty_mags: false` to `true`, restricted `RefillSoldierPrimaryAndStowedWeapons` strictly to genuinely dry weapon slots (`IsOutOfAmmosFor`) with weight-headroom checks, prevented Buddy Resupply (`BorrowAmmos`) double-stacking onto `forceRefillQueue`, deferred weapon-pickup refills until after async `PickUpCR` equips the new gun (stripping orphaned magazines from the dropped weapon first), filtered floor `Ammo`/`Magazine` scavenging by held-gun compatibility (`compatibleAmmo`, `Magazine.socket`, `socketCompatibilityMap`) and `CanSoldierAcceptExtraMass`, gated `AddVirtualItem`/`TakeIntoInventory` weight additions, and added an O(1)-throttled `TickBotInventoryWeightAndDelayedWeaponRefill` audit (`PruneBotInventoryForSprintWeight`) that sheds surplus reserves whenever `weight > max_weight`.
* **20-Player Co-op Stress-Test Fixes (`BallisticsAndImmersionModule.cs`, `MacroAndVehiclesModule.cs`, `FeatureMenuAndHud.cs`, `AiCore.cs`, `Plugin.cs`)**:
  * **Post-Mortem Projectile & AP Penetration Fix (`Soldier_Say_Patch`, `Soldier_SyncSay_Patch`, `BulletInstance_OnHit_Patch.Finalizer`, `VehicleDamagablePart_TryPenetrateArmor_Patch.Finalizer`)**: Stopped nulling `BulletInstance.shooter` when shooters died/despawned mid-flight—preserving `shooter` for post-mortem AP tank/AT armor penetration and helmet-headshot checks while guarding `Soldier.Say` / `Soldier.SyncSay` directly (`10` NREs eliminated), and wrapped impact/spall state cleanup in Harmony `Finalizer`s.
  * **Co-op Wiped-Vehicle Spotting NRE Fix (`ResourcesManager_TrySpotSquad_Patch`)**: Guarded `ResourcesManager.TrySpotSquad` (`0x18072C6AE`) against null `Squad.Leader` or `SyncSoldier` when spotting an enemy vehicle mid-wipe in Co-op, falling back cleanly to local `Squad.MarkSquad`.
  * **`F5` Cold Mission Budget & `Sap:` Persistence**: Added a `2.0ms` per-frame budget and `UnitCountsResolved` guard to `StepResolveSelectedCampaignBattles` / `EnsureEntryUnitCountsResolved` so cold `sp_ME_` mission loads resolve without multi-load hitches or infinite retries, and preserved cumulative `SpecialMunitionsUsedCount` (`Sap:`) across map transitions.
